Historical strength estimates (17 November): two copies of the card go into its family's starter deck, which then plays 1,600 games against eight test rivals. The number is how many points of win rate the card adds (+) or costs (−). 0 means as good as a basic starter card, so a plain 1/2 unit scores higher than a plain 1/1. Rarity targets: Common 0, Uncommon +3, Rare +6, Mythic +10, Legendary +15. Rival decks are still built with rarity points (Common 0 … Legendary 15) until they are rebuilt by strength.
